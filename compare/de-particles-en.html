的 vs 得 vs 地: How to Use the Three "De" in Mandarin

Intermediate台湾華語Read in another language: 日本語 · 한국어

的, 得, and 地 are all pronounced "de" in everyday speech, but each does a different grammatical job. Mixing them up is one of the most common mistakes even intermediate learners make — here's how to tell them apart for good.

Quick comparison

ParticleGrammatical rolePositionExample
的Marks possession or describes a nounBefore a noun我的書 (my book)
的Links a description to a nounBefore a noun漂亮的衣服 (pretty clothes)
得Marks degree or result after a verbAfter a verb, before a description跑得快 (runs fast)
得Shows ability (can/cannot)After a verb看得懂 (can understand by looking)
地Turns a descriptive word into an adverbBefore a verb慢慢地走 (walk slowly)
地Marks the manner of an actionBefore a verb高興地說 (said happily)

的 — Possession and Description

的 is by far the most common of the three and the one most learners meet first. It attaches a description or an owner to the noun that follows it: 我的書 (my book), 漂亮的衣服 (pretty clothes). If you're unsure which "de" to use, 的 is usually the safe default right before a noun.

得 — After a Verb, Describing the Result

得 shows up right after a verb to introduce how the action goes or what its result is: 跑得快 (runs fast), 說得很清楚 (speaks very clearly). It's also used to express ability — 看得懂 means "can understand by looking/reading."

地 — Turning a Description Into an Adverb

地 goes between a descriptive word and the verb it modifies, functioning like "-ly" in English: 慢慢地走 (walk slowly), 高興地說 (said happily). It only appears before verbs, never before nouns, which is the easiest way to rule it out when deciding between the three.

The Fastest Way to Tell Them Apart

Ask what comes right after the particle. A noun next means 的. A verb followed by a description of how it happened means 得. A verb by itself, with the description placed before it, means 地. Getting these three right is one of the clearest signals of a more advanced learner, so it's worth drilling early.

Examples

我的書
wǒ de shū
my book
他跑得很快
tā pǎo de hěn kuài
He runs very fast
慢慢地走
màn màn de zǒu
walk slowly
高興地說
gāo xìng de shuō
said happily
